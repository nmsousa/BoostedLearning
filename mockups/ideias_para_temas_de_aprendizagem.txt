Beginner (A1 – Foundation & Survival)

Focus: Essential greetings, basic nouns, numbers, and immediate daily needs.
• Unit 1: Hello & Goodbye (Greetings, polite expressions, introducing yourself)
• Unit 2: First Words (Common everyday objects, genders/articles, plural forms)
• Unit 3: Numbers & Time (Counting 1–100, telling time, days of the week, months)
• Unit 4: Getting Around (Directions, public transport, asking "Where is...?")
• Unit 5: Food & Drink (Ordering at a cafe/restaurant, common ingredients, dietary likes/dislikes)
• Unit 6: Shopping & Money (Asking for prices, sizes, colors, paying in a store)

Elementary (A2 – Everyday Life & Routine)

Focus: Simple past/future tenses, daily habits, family, and basic social interactions.
• Unit 7: My Family & Friends (Describing people, physical appearance, personality traits)
• Unit 8: Daily Routines (Morning/evening habits, jobs, hobbies, free-time activities)
• Unit 9: Home & Neighborhood (Rooms in a house, furniture, chores, local landmarks)
• Unit 10: Weather & Seasons (Describing climate, planning outdoor activities, clothing)
• Unit 11: Health & the Body (Body parts, seeing a doctor, pharmacy, talking about how you feel)
• Unit 12: Travel & Hotel (Checking into a hotel, airport vocabulary, booking tickets)

Intermediate (B1 – Practical Independence)

Focus: Expressing opinions, handling unexpected situations, past storytelling, and work.
• Unit 13: Making Plans (Invitations, accepting/declining, making reservations, phone calls)
• Unit 14: Work & Studies (Office life, interviews, school subjects, writing emails)
• Unit 15: Food & Cooking (Recipes, kitchen tools, describing tastes, hosting guests)
• Unit 16: Tech & Communication (Using a smartphone, social media, internet slang, fixing gadgets)
• Unit 17: Stories & Memories (Narrating past events, childhood memories, travel highlights)
• Unit 18: Problems & Solutions (Reporting lost items, complaining politely, car trouble)

Upper-Intermediate (B2 – Fluency & Nuance)

Focus: Abstract topics, debate, media consumption, and social/cultural contexts.
• Unit 19: Opinions & Debates (Agreeing/disagreeing, expressing doubt, formal vs. informal tone)
• Unit 20: Culture & Traditions (Festivals, local customs, art, music, history)
• Unit 21: News & Media (Reading headlines, talking about current events, digital privacy)
• Unit 22: Environment & Nature (Climate issues, wildlife, city vs. country life)
• Unit 23: Money & Careers (Banking, budgeting, job growth, negotiations)
• Unit 24: Humor & Idioms (Local slang, cultural jokes, common metaphors, sarcasm)
